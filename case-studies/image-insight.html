About

Bringing coaching cards online

Sometimes it’s easier to start with a picture than find the words. Image Insight brings physical coaching cards into a digital session for coaches and therapists working with adults and children.

The coach asks a question and the participant picks images to talk about. Reflections and next steps are saved in a downloadable PDF.

The co-founder brought me in with a working prototype. My role was to bring some finesse to the interface and improve the workflow.

Approach

A calm environment for reflection

I kept the original concept, adding more breathing room, softer colours and clearer actions. The aim was to help people focus on the images and feelings.

User feedback

Refining around real sessions

A beta with two schools helped us see what worked and what needed adjusting.

Starting a session

Coaches wanted to start a session straight away. The app’s developer suggested a separate instant-session button; I suggested one entry point where coaches could start now or schedule for later.

Controlling the image grid

A large grid could feel overwhelming, including for some neurodivergent children and young people. I added a slider to adjust the number of images and placed it over the grid so coaches could see the effect as they adjusted it.

A gallery selector and refresh button let coaches switch collections or try a fresh batch.
